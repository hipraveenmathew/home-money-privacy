Home Money – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Home Money is a calculator app for planning a home purchase: saving for a deposit, comparing mortgages, renting versus buying, and investing. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

The short version

  • There is no account, no sign-up, no adverts and no tracking.
  • Everything you type stays on your device.
  • The app only connects to the internet to check for app updates, or when you choose to share, open a link or ask an AI assistant.

Information stored on your device

The numbers you enter, saved scenarios, saved links and notes, the name you choose to be greeted by, your settings and an error log are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We cannot see them. Your device may include this data in its own encrypted backup (for example to your Google account), which is controlled by you and your device provider. Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage in your phone's settings) deletes all of this data. Inside the app you can also reset the calculators, clear the error log, and delete saved scenarios and links individually.

Information that leaves your device

  • App update check. When the app starts it asks our update provider, Expo (650 Industries, Inc.), whether a newer version is available. This request contains technical information only: the app version, your device platform and a random installation identifier created by the app. It contains nothing you have typed and is not used for advertising or tracking. See Expo's privacy policy.
  • Things you choose to do. If you tap Share, create a PDF, open a saved link or use "Ask AI", the information shown on screen is passed to the app or website you pick (for example WhatsApp, your email app, ChatGPT or Claude). The "Ask AI" screen shows exactly what will be shared before you send it. Those services' own privacy policies then apply.

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, and the app contains no analytics or advertising software.

The web app

Home Money is also available as a web app that you can open in a browser or add to your home screen. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any website, opening it downloads the app's files, and GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address (see GitHub's privacy statement). The web app does not use the Expo update check. Everything you type is stored only in your browser on your device, and clearing your browser's data for the site deletes it.

Children

The app is intended for adults planning their finances and is not directed at children.

Estimates, not advice

Results are estimates to help you explore options. They are not financial advice.
